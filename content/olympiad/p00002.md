<p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00002/">Problem 00002</a></p> 
<p>Let \(n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}\) and let \(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_n>0\). Prove that:</p>
<p class="mpc">\[S=\frac{x_1}{x_2}+\frac{x_2}{x_3}+\dots+\frac{x_{n-1}}{x_n}+\frac{x_n}{x_1} \ge n \]</p>
<details>
    <summary>Hint 1</summary>
    <p>What happens if you take the product of all the terms in the sum?</p>
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Solution</summary>
    <p>To prove the inequality, we apply the AM-GM Inequality:</p>
    <p>\[ 
        \frac{S}{n} \ge \sqrt[n]{\frac{x_1}{x_2}\frac{x_2}{x_3}\dots\frac{x_n}{x_1}} \Leftrightarrow 
        S \ge n\cdot\sqrt[n]{1} \Leftrightarrow
        S \ge n
    \]
    </p>
    <p>Equality holds if and only if \(x_1 = x_2 = \dots = x_n\).</p>
</details>
