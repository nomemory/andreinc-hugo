<div class="mp" id="p00031">
        <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00031/">Problem 00031</a></p> 
        <p>Let \(a,b,c\) be positive real numbers. Prove that:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \sqrt{\frac{ab}{ab+c(a+b+c)}}+\sqrt{\frac{bc}{bc+a(a+b+c)}}+\sqrt{\frac{ca}{ca+b(a+b+c)}} \leq \frac{3}{2}
            \]
        </p>
    <details>
        <summary>Hint 1</summary>
        <p>Regroup and factor the denominator of each term. In particular, observe that:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \sqrt{\frac{ab}{ab+c(a+b+c)}} = \sqrt{ab} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{ab+ca+cb+c^2}} = \sqrt{ab} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{(a+c)(b+c)}}
            \]
        </p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Solution</summary>
        <p>After factoring the denominators, we can rewrite the original inequality as:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \sqrt{\frac{ab}{ab+c(a+b+c)}}+\sqrt{\frac{bc}{bc+a(a+b+c)}}+\sqrt{\frac{ca}{ca+b(a+b+c)}} = \\
                \frac{\sqrt{ab}}{\sqrt{(a+c)(b+c)}}+\frac{\sqrt{bc}}{\sqrt{(a+b)(a+c)}}+\frac{\sqrt{ca}}{\sqrt{(a+b)(b+c)}}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Next, we apply the Cauchy–Bunyakovsky–Schwarz (CBS) inequality:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \frac{\sqrt{ab}}{\sqrt{(a+c)(b+c)}}+\frac{\sqrt{bc}}{\sqrt{(a+b)(a+c)}}+\frac{\sqrt{ca}}{\sqrt{(a+b)(b+c)}} \leq \\
                \sqrt{(ab+bc+ca)\left(\frac{1}{(a+c)(b+c)}+\frac{1}{(a+b)(a+c)}+\frac{1}{(a+b)(b+c)}\right)}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Therefore, it suffices to prove:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \sqrt{\left(\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}} ab\right)\cdot\left[\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}\frac{1}{(a+b)(b+c)}\right]} \overbrace{\leq}^{?} \frac{3}{2} \Leftrightarrow
            \]
            \[
                \sqrt{(ab+bc+ca)}\cdot\sqrt{\frac{1}{(a+b)(b+c)}+\frac{1}{(b+c)(c+a)}+\frac{1}{(c+a)(a+b)}} \overbrace{\leq}^{?} \frac{3}{2}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Squaring both sides, this becomes the more tractable condition:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                8\cdot(ab+bc+ca)(a+b+c) \overbrace{\leq}^{?} 9 \cdot (a+b)(b+c)(c+a)
            \]
        </p>
        <p>After further simplification or factorization it leads to:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                (ab+bc+ca)(a+b+c) \overbrace{\geq}^{?} 9\cdot abc
            \]
        </p>
        <p>The final inequality follows directly from the identity</p>
            <p class="mpc">
            \[
                (a+b+c)(ab+bc+ca)-9abc
                =a(b-c)^2+b(c-a)^2+c(a-b)^2\ge0.
            \]
            </p>
            <p>Equality holds if and only if \(a=b=c\).</p>
    </details>
