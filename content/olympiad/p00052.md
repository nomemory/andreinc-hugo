<div class="mp" id="p00052">
        <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00052/">Problem 00052</a></p> 
        <p>Let \( x, y, z \) be positive real numbers. Prove the following inequality:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \left(1+\frac{x}{y}\right)\cdot\left(1+\frac{y}{z}\right)\cdot\left(1+\frac{z}{x}\right) \geq 2 + 2\cdot\frac{x+y+z}{\sqrt[3]{xyz}}
            \]
        </p>
        <details>
            <summary>Hint 1</summary>
            <p>The term \(\sqrt[3]{xyz}\) suggests the use of the AM-GM inequality. Try to simplify or restructure the expression to reveal an appropriate application.</p>
        </details>
        <details>
            <summary>Solution</summary>
            <p>We start by simplifying the left-hand side:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \left(1+\frac{x}{y}\right)\cdot\left(1+\frac{y}{z}\right)\cdot\left(1+\frac{z}{x}\right) = 
                \]
                \[
                    = \left(1+\frac{y}{z}+\frac{x}{y}+\frac{xy}{yz}\right)\left(1+\frac{z}{x}\right) = 
                \]
                \[
                    = 1 + \frac{z}{x} + \frac{y}{z} + \frac{y}{x} + \frac{x}{y} + \frac{z}{y} + \frac{x}{z} + 1 = 
                \]
                \[
                    = 2+\left(\frac{x}{y}+\frac{y}{z}+\frac{z}{x}\right)
                    +\left(\frac{y}{x}+\frac{z}{y}+\frac{x}{z}\right). \tag{1}
                \]
            </p>
            <p>We now estimate each of the two cyclic sums in \((1)\). Multiplying the first one by \(3\) and regrouping gives:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    3\left(\frac{x}{y}+\frac{y}{z}+\frac{z}{x}\right)
                    =\left(\frac{x}{y}+\frac{x}{y}+\frac{y}{z}\right)
                    +\left(\frac{y}{z}+\frac{y}{z}+\frac{z}{x}\right)
                    +\left(\frac{z}{x}+\frac{z}{x}+\frac{x}{y}\right).
                \]
            </p>
            <p>Applying AM-GM to each group:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    3\left(\frac{x}{y}+\frac{y}{z}+\frac{z}{x}\right)
                    \ge3\sqrt[3]{\frac{x^2}{yz}}+3\sqrt[3]{\frac{y^2}{zx}}+3\sqrt[3]{\frac{z^2}{xy}}
                    =3\cdot\frac{x+y+z}{\sqrt[3]{xyz}}.
                \]
            </p>
            <p>Thus,</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \frac{x}{y}+\frac{y}{z}+\frac{z}{x}\ge\frac{x+y+z}{\sqrt[3]{xyz}}. \tag{2}
                \]
            </p>
            <p>Applying the same grouping in the reverse cyclic order gives:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \frac{y}{x}+\frac{z}{y}+\frac{x}{z}\ge\frac{x+y+z}{\sqrt[3]{xyz}}. \tag{3}
                \]
            </p>
            <p>Combining \((1)\), \((2)\), and \((3)\) proves the desired inequality. Equality holds if and only if \(x=y=z\).</p>
        </details>
