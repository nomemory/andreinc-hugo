<p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00066/">Problem 00066</a></p> 
<p>Let \(a,b,c\) be positive real numbers. Prove that:</p>
<p class="mpc">
    \[ \frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2+8bc}} + \frac{b}{\sqrt{b^2+8ac}} + \frac{c}{\sqrt{c^2+8ab}} \ge 1. \]
</p>
<details>
    <summary>Solution</summary>
    <p>Using the Cauchy-Bunyakovsky-Schwarz inequality, we obtain:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}\frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2+8bc}}
            =\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}\frac{a^2}{a\sqrt{a^2+8bc}}
            \geq \frac{(a+b+c)^2}{\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}a\sqrt{a^2+8bc}}. \tag{1}
        \]
    </p>
    <p>We estimate the denominator using the Cauchy-Bunyakovsky-Schwarz inequality once more:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \left(\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}a\sqrt{a^2+8bc}\right)^2
            \leq (a+b+c)\left(a^3+b^3+c^3+24abc\right). \tag{2}
        \]
    </p>
    <p>By AM-GM applied to the six terms \(a^2b,a^2c,b^2a,b^2c,c^2a,c^2b\),</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            a^2b+a^2c+b^2a+b^2c+c^2a+c^2b\geq6abc.
        \]
    </p>
    <p>Consequently:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            (a+b+c)^3
            =a^3+b^3+c^3+3\sum_{\mathrm{sym}}a^2b+6abc
            \geq a^3+b^3+c^3+24abc. \tag{3}
        \]
    </p>
    <p>It follows from \((2)\) and \((3)\) that \(\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}a\sqrt{a^2+8bc}\leq(a+b+c)^2\). Substituting this into \((1)\) proves the required inequality. Equality holds if and only if \(a=b=c\).</p>
</details>
