<div class="mp" id="p00067">
    <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00067/">Problem 00067</a></p> 
    <p>If \(x\in\mathbb{R}\), solve:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            |x^2-9|+|x^2-16| \lt 47
        \]
    </p>
<details> 
    <summary>Graphical Solution</summary>
    <p><img src="/images/2025-12-09-15-A-short-introduction-to-math-olympiad-inequalities/p01.png" alt="Graph of the two sides of the inequation"/></p>
</details>
<details>
    <summary>Algebraic Solution</summary>
    <p>We distinguish three cases according to the value of \(x^2\):</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            |x^2-9|+|x^2-16|=
            \begin{cases}
                25-2x^2, & x^2\leq9,\\
                7, & 9\leq x^2\leq16,\\
                2x^2-25, & x^2\geq16.
            \end{cases}
        \]
    </p>
    <p>The first two expressions are automatically less than \(47\). In the third case, the inequality is equivalent to \(2x^2-25\lt47\), or \(x^2\lt36\). Thus all solutions are:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            x\in(-6,6).
        \]
    </p>
</details>
