<div class="mp" id="p00068">
    <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00068/">Problem 00068</a></p> 
    <p>Find real numbers \(x\) for which the following "inequality" holds:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \sqrt{3-x}-\sqrt{x+1} \gt \frac{1}{2}
        \]
    </p>
    <details>
        <summary>Solution</summary>
        <p>The radicals require \(-1\leq x\leq3\). Since both sides of</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \sqrt{3-x}\gt\sqrt{x+1}+\frac12
            \]
        </p>
        <p>are non-negative, squaring preserves the inequality:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                3-x\gt x+1+\sqrt{x+1}+\frac14
                \Leftrightarrow \frac74-2x\gt\sqrt{x+1}. \tag{1}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Set \(t=\sqrt{x+1}\geq0\), so \(x=t^2-1\). Inequality \((1)\) becomes:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                8t^2+4t-15\lt0.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>The positive root of the corresponding quadratic equation is \(\frac{\sqrt{31}-1}{4}\). Hence:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                0\leq t\lt\frac{\sqrt{31}-1}{4}
                \Leftrightarrow
                -1\leq x\lt1-\frac{\sqrt{31}}8.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Therefore the solution set is \(\left[-1,1-\frac{\sqrt{31}}8\right)\).</p>
    </details>
