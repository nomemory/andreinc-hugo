<p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00082/">Problem 00082</a></p> 
<p>Let \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) and \(n \gt 1\). Prove that:</p>
<p class="mpc">\[\frac{1}{2}\lt\frac{1}{n+1}+\frac{1}{n+2}+\dots+\frac{1}{2n}\lt\frac{3}{4}\]</p>
<details> 
    <summary>Hint 1</summary>
    <p>\(\frac{1}{n+j}\gt\frac{1}{2n}, \forall j \lt n\)</p>
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Solution</summary>
    <p>To find a lower bound, each term satisfies \(\frac{1}{n+j} \ge \frac{1}{2n}\), and the inequality is strict when \(j\lt n\). Since \(n\gt1\), summing gives:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \frac{1}{n+1}+\frac{1}{n+2}+\dots+\frac{1}{2n}\gt\frac{1}{2n}+\frac{1}{2n}+\dots+\frac{1}{2n}=\frac{1}{2}
        \]
    </p>
    <p>For the upper bound, pair the sum with the same terms written in reverse order:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            S=\frac12\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left(\frac{1}{n+j}+\frac{1}{2n+1-j}\right). \tag{1}
        \]
    </p>
    <p>For \(1\leq j\leq n\),</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            (n+j)(2n+1-j)-2n(n+1)=(j-1)(n-j)\geq0.
        \]
    </p>
    <p>Therefore:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \frac{1}{n+j}+\frac{1}{2n+1-j}
            =\frac{3n+1}{(n+j)(2n+1-j)}
            \leq\frac{3n+1}{2n(n+1)}
            \lt\frac{3}{2n}. \tag{2}
        \]
    </p>
    <p>Substituting \((2)\) into \((1)\) yields \(S\lt\frac12\cdot n\cdot\frac{3}{2n}=\frac34\).</p>
    <p>Thus, we have:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \frac{1}{2} \lt \sum_{j=1}^n \frac{1}{n+j} \lt \frac{3}{4}
        \]
    </p>
    <p>which completes the proof.</p>
</details>
