<div class="mp" id="p00084">
        <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00084/">Problem 00084</a></p> 
        <p>Let \(x\in\mathbb{R}_{+}\) and \(n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}\). Define:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                R_1=\sqrt{x^2+x},\qquad R_{k+1}=\sqrt{x^2+x+R_k}\quad(k\geq1).
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Prove that \(R_n\lt x+1\).</p>
        <details>
            <summary>Hint 1</summary>
            <p>Try defining the expression recursively after squaring both sides.</p>
        </details>
        <details>
            <summary>Solution</summary>
            <p>We prove the inequality by induction. For \(n=1\), both sides are non-negative, so:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \sqrt{x^2+x} < x + 1 \Leftrightarrow x^2 + x < x^2 + 2x + 1 \Leftrightarrow 0 < x + 1
                \]
            </p>
            <p>This is true because \(x\geq0\). Now suppose \(R_k\lt x+1\). Then:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    R_{k+1}^2=x^2+x+R_k
                    \lt x^2+2x+1=(x+1)^2.
                \]
            </p>
            <p>Since \(R_{k+1}\geq0\) and \(x+1\gt0\), it follows that \(R_{k+1}\lt x+1\). This completes the induction.</p>
        </details>
        <details>
            <summary>Source</summary>
            <p>D.M. Bătineţu-Giurgiu</p>
        </details>
