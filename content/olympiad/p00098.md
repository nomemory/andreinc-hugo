<p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00098/">Problem 00098</a></p> 
<p>Let \(a,b,c,x,y,z\) be positive real numbers such that \(x+y+z=a+b+c=1\). Prove that:</p>
<p class="mpc">
\[
    \frac{1}{ax+by+cz}+\frac{1}{cx+ay+bz}+\frac{1}{bx+cy+az} \ge 9
\]
</p>
<details>
    <summary>Hint 1</summary>
    <p>Can the HM-AM inequality help in solving this problem?</p>
</details>
<details>
    <summary>Solution</summary>
    <p>We apply the HM-AM inequality in the following manner:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
    \[
        \frac{\sum\frac{1}{ax+by+cz}}{3} \ge \frac{3}{(ax+by+cz)+(cx+ay+bz)+(bx+cy+az)} \Leftrightarrow \\
        \sum\frac{1}{ax+by+cz} \ge \frac{9}{a(x+y+z)+b(x+y+z)+c(x+y+z)} \Leftrightarrow \\
        \sum\frac{1}{ax+by+cz} \ge \frac{9}{(a+b+c)(x+y+z)} \Leftrightarrow \\
        \sum\frac{1}{ax+by+cz} \ge 9
    \]
    </p>
    <p>For the equality case, write the three denominators as \(D_1=ax+by+cz\), \(D_2=cx+ay+bz\), and \(D_3=bx+cy+az\). A direct expansion gives:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            (D_1-D_2)^2+(D_2-D_3)^2+(D_3-D_1)^2
            =\frac12\left((a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2\right)
            \left((x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2\right).
        \]
    </p>
    <p>Equality in HM-AM means \(D_1=D_2=D_3\), so the identity shows that either \(a=b=c\) or \(x=y=z\). Using the two sum conditions, equality holds precisely when \(a=b=c=\frac13\) or \(x=y=z=\frac13\).</p>
</details>
