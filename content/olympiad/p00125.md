<div class="mp" id="p00125">
        <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00125/">Problem 00125</a></p> 
        <p>Let \(a, b, c\) be the lengths of the sides of a triangle. Prove the following inequality:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \frac{a+c-b}{a+2b-c}+\frac{a+b-c}{b+2c-a}+\frac{b+c-a}{c+2a-b} \ge \frac{3}{2}
            \]
        </p>
        <details>
            <summary>Hint 1</summary>
            <p>The structure of the inequality and the presence of \( \frac{3}{2} \) on the right-hand side suggest a potential connection to the Nesbitt inequality. Can you think of useful substitutions to simplify the expression?</p>
        </details>
        <details>
            <summary>Hint 2</summary>
            <p>Consider using Ravi substitution, which is often helpful for triangle-related inequalities. The substitution is as follows:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \begin{cases}
                        a = x + y \\
                        b = y + z \\
                        c = z + x
                    \end{cases}
                \]
            </p>
        </details>
        <details>
            <summary>Solution</summary>
            <p>By the strict triangle inequalities, Ravi's substitution \(a=x+y\), \(b=y+z\), and \(c=z+x\) gives \(x,y,z>0\). The inequality transforms into:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \frac{2x}{3y+z} + \frac{2y}{3z+x} + \frac{2z}{3x+y} \ge \frac{3}{2}.
                \]
            </p>
            <p>This is exactly the refinement proved in the preceding problem, with the parameters \(a=\frac32\) and \(b=\frac12\). Therefore:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \frac{x}{\frac32y+\frac12z}+
                    \frac{y}{\frac32z+\frac12x}+
                    \frac{z}{\frac32x+\frac12y}
                    \ge \frac{3}{\frac32+\frac12}=\frac32.
                \]
            </p>
            <p>Equality holds when \(x=y=z\), which is equivalent to \(a=b=c\), so the triangle is equilateral.</p>
        </details>
