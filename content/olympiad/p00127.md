<div class="mp" id="p00127">
        <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00127/">Problem 00127</a></p> 
        <p>Let \(a,b,c\) be real numbers greater than or equal to \(1\), and let \(k\in\mathbb{R}_{+}\). Prove that:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \frac{a}{k+b+c}+\frac{b}{k+c+a}+\frac{c}{k+a+b} \ge \frac{3}{k+2}
            \]
        </p>
    <details>
        <summary>Hint 1</summary>
        <p>Try applying the Cauchy-Bunyakovsky-Schwarz (CBS) inequality.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Solution</summary>
        <p>First, apply the CBS inequality:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \left(\frac{a}{k+b+c}+\frac{b}{k+c+a}+\frac{c}{k+a+b}\right)
                \left(a(k+b+c)+b(k+c+a)+c(k+a+b)\right)
                \ge (a+b+c)^2.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Rearranging, we obtain:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \frac{a}{k+b+c}+\frac{b}{k+c+a}+\frac{c}{k+a+b} \geq \frac{(a+b+c)^2}{k(a+b+c)+2(ab+bc+ca)}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Since \(a,b,c \ge 1\), we have \(a+b+c\le ab+bc+ca\). Because \(k\ge0\), it follows that:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                k(a+b+c)+2(ab+bc+ca)\le(k+2)(ab+bc+ca).
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Moreover, \((a+b+c)^2\ge3(ab+bc+ca)\). Therefore:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \frac{(a+b+c)^2}{k(a+b+c)+2(ab+bc+ca)}
                \ge\frac{(a+b+c)^2}{(k+2)(ab+bc+ca)}
                \ge\frac{3}{k+2}.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>The inequality is proven. If \(k>0\), equality holds exactly when \(a=b=c=1\). If \(k=0\) is included in \(\mathbb R_+\), equality holds exactly when \(a=b=c\ge1\).</p>
    </details>
