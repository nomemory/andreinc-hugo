<div class="mp" id="p00138">
        <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00138/">Problem 00138</a></p> 
        <p>Let \(a,b,c\) be positive real numbers such that \(a+b+c=1\). Prove that:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \frac{\log^2_{ab}c}{a+b} + \frac{\log^2_{bc}a}{b+c}+\frac{\log^2_{ca}b}{c+a} \geq \frac{9}{8}
            \]
        </p>
        <details>
            <summary>Solution</summary>
            <p>By Titu's lemma (a direct consequence of Cauchy-Schwarz inequality), we have:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \frac{\log^2_{ab}c}{a+b} + \frac{\log^2_{bc}a}{b+c}+\frac{\log^2_{ca}b}{c+a} \geq \frac{(\log_{ab}c+\log_{bc}a+\log_{ca}b)^2}{2(a+b+c)} =
                \]
                \[
                    = \frac{(\log_{ab}c+\log_{bc}a+\log_{ca}b)^2}{2} \tag{2}
                \]
            </p>
             <p>Since \(a,b,c\in(0,1)\), the numbers \(A=-\ln a\), \(B=-\ln b\), and \(C=-\ln c\) are positive. Changing bases turns the logarithmic sum into Nesbitt's inequality:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \log_{ab}c+\log_{bc}a+\log_{ca}b
                    =\frac{C}{A+B}+\frac{A}{B+C}+\frac{B}{C+A}
                    \geq \frac{3}{2}. \tag{1}
                \]
            </p>
            <p>Combining inequalities (1) and (2) proves our inequality. Equality holds exactly when \(a=b=c=\frac13\).</p>
        </details>
