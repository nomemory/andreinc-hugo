<div class="mp" id="p00143">
        <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00143/">Problem 00143</a></p> 
        <p>Let \(n\) be a positive integer and let \(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n\) be positive real numbers such that \(\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{1}{x_i}=k\), where \(k\) is a fixed positive real number. Prove that:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i+\frac{1}{x_i})^2 \ge \frac{(n^2+k^2)^2}{nk^2}
            \]
        </p>
        <details>
            <summary>Hint 1</summary>
            <p>We can apply Titu's Lemma in the following manner:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{(x_i+\frac{1}{x_i})^2}{1} \ge \frac{(\sum_{i=1}^n x_i + \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{x_i})^2}{\underbrace{1+\dots+1}_{n}}
            \]
            </p>
        </details>
        <details>
            <summary>Hint 2</summary>
            <p>Prove:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \sum_{i=1}^nx_i \ge \frac{n^2}{k}
                \]
            </p>
        </details>
        <details>
            <summary>Solution</summary>
            <p>First, the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality gives:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \left(\sum_{i=1}^n x_i\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^n\frac1{x_i}\right)\ge n^2,
                    \qquad\text{so}\qquad
                    \sum_{i=1}^n x_i\ge\frac{n^2}{k}.
                \]
            </p>
            <p>Now apply Titu's Lemma and use this bound:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(x_i+\frac{1}{x_i}\right)^2
                    \ge \frac{\left(\sum_{i=1}^n x_i+\sum_{i=1}^n\frac1{x_i}\right)^2}{n}
                    =\frac{\left(\sum_{i=1}^n x_i+k\right)^2}{n}
                    \ge\frac{\left(\frac{n^2}{k}+k\right)^2}{n}
                    =\frac{(n^2+k^2)^2}{nk^2}.
                \]
            </p>
            <p>Equality holds exactly when \(x_1=\dots=x_n=\frac{n}{k}\).</p>
        </details>

        </details>
