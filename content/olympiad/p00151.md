<div class="mp" id="p00151">
        <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00151/">Problem 00151</a></p> 
        <p>Let \(a,b,c,d>0\). Prove that:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                (a+c)^c(b+d)^d(c+d)^{c+d} \le c^c d^d(a+b+c+d)^{c+d}
            \]
        </p>
        <details>
            <summary>Hint 1</summary>
            <p>Group the terms by their powers.</p>
        </details>
        <details>
            <summary>Hint 2</summary>
            <p>By "regrouping the terms", the new inequality becomes:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \frac{(a+c)^c}{c^c}\cdot\frac{(b+d)^d}{d^d} \le \frac{(a+b+c+d)^{c+d}}{(c+d)^{c+d}}
                \]
            </p>
        </details>
        <details>
            <summary>Solution</summary>
            <p>By "regrouping the terms" based on their corresponding powers, the inequality is equivalent to:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \Bigl( \frac{a+b+c+d}{c+d} \Bigr)^{c+d} \ge \Bigl( \frac{a}{c}+1 \Bigr)^c \Bigl( \frac{b}{d} + 1 \Bigr)^d
                \]
            </p>
            <p>At the same time, if we apply the weighted AM-GM inequality to \( \bigl(\frac{a}{c}+1\bigr), \bigl(\frac{b}{d}+1\bigr)\), with weights \(w_1=c\) and \(w_2=d\), we obtain:</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                    \frac{c\left(\frac{a}{c}+1\right)+d\left(\frac{b}{d}+1\right)}{c+d} \ge \left[\left(\frac{a}{c}+1\right)^c\left(\frac{b}{d}+1\right)^d\right]^{\frac{1}{c+d}} \Leftrightarrow \\
                    \frac{a+b+c+d}{c+d} \ge \left[\left(\frac{a}{c}+1\right)^c\left(\frac{b}{d}+1\right)^d\right]^{\frac{1}{c+d}}
                \]
            </p>
            <p>After raising both sides to the positive power \(c+d\):</p>
            <p class="mpc">
                \[
                      \Bigl( \frac{a+b+c+d}{c+d} \Bigr)^{c+d} \ge \Bigl(\frac{a}{c}+1\Bigr)^c\Bigl(\frac{b}{d}+1\Bigr)^d
                \]
            </p>
            <p>Equality holds exactly when \(\frac{a}{c}=\frac{b}{d}\).</p>
        </details>
