<div class="mp" id="p00169">
    <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00169/">Problem 00169</a></p>
    <p>For $a,b,c \in (0,1)$, $x,y,z > 0$, if $a^x=bc, b^y=ca, c^z=ab$, prove $\frac{1}{2+x} + \frac{1}{2+y} + \frac{1}{2+z} \le \frac{3}{4}$.</p>
    <details>
        <summary>Hint</summary>
        <p>Work on the expressions involving logarithms. Consider changing the base to a common one.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Solution</summary>
        <p>Let $A=-\ln a$, $B=-\ln b$, and $C=-\ln c$. These numbers are positive because $a,b,c\in(0,1)$. Taking logarithms in the three given equations gives:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[ x=\frac{B+C}{A}, \qquad y=\frac{C+A}{B}, \qquad z=\frac{A+B}{C}. \]
        </p>
        <p>Set $T=A+B+C$. Then:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[ \frac{1}{2+x}=\frac{A}{A+T}=1-\frac{T}{A+T}, \]
        </p>
        <p>and similarly for the other two terms. By the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \frac{1}{A+T}+\frac{1}{B+T}+\frac{1}{C+T}
                \ge\frac{9}{A+B+C+3T}=\frac{9}{4T}.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Consequently:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \frac{1}{2+x}+\frac{1}{2+y}+\frac{1}{2+z}
                =3-T\left(\frac{1}{A+T}+\frac{1}{B+T}+\frac{1}{C+T}\right)
                \le3-\frac94=\frac34.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Equality holds exactly when $A=B=C$, equivalently when $a=b=c$ and $x=y=z=2$.</p>
    </details>
