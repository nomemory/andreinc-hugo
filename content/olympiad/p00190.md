<p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00190/">Problem 00190</a></p>
<p>Let \(a,b>0\), with \(a,b\ne1\). Solve the equation</p>
<p>
    \[
        (\log_a x)^{\log_b x}=(\log_b x)^{\log_a x},
    \]
</p>
<p>for \(x>0\) such that \(\log_a x>0\) and \(\log_b x>0\). These conditions ensure that both real powers are defined in the usual sense.</p>
<details>
    <summary>Hint</summary>
    <p>Set \(u=\log_a x\), \(v=\log_b x\), and compare \(v\) with \(u\) by changing bases.</p>
</details>
<details>
    <summary>Solution</summary>
    <p>Put</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            u=\log_a x>0,\qquad v=\log_b x>0,\qquad
            r=\frac{\ln a}{\ln b}.
        \]
    </p>
    <p>By the change-of-base formula, \(v=ru\). In particular, the domain conditions force \(r>0\), so \(a\) and \(b\) must lie on the same side of \(1\). The equation becomes</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            u^{ru}=(ru)^u.
        \]
    </p>
    <p>Since \(u>0\), taking the positive \(u\)-th root gives</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            u^r=ru.
        \]
    </p>
    <p>If \(a=b\), then \(r=1\), the two sides of the original equation are identical, and every admissible \(x\) is a solution.</p>
    <p>If \(a\ne b\), then \(r\ne1\), and</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            u^{r-1}=r
            \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
            u=r^{\frac{1}{r-1}}.
        \]
    </p>
    <p>Because \(u=\log_a x\), the unique solution in this case is</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \boxed{x=a^{\,r^{1/(r-1)}}},
            \qquad r=\frac{\ln a}{\ln b}.
        \]
    </p>
    <p>If \(a\) and \(b\) lie on opposite sides of \(1\), there is no \(x\) satisfying the stated positivity conditions. Notice that \(x=1\) is not admissible: it would turn both sides into the indeterminate expression \(0^0\).</p>
</details>
