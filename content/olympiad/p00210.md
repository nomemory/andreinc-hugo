<div class="mp" id="p00210">
    <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00210/">Problem 00210</a></p>
    <p>Let \(a,b,c\) be positive real numbers such that \(a^2+b^2+c^2=1\). Prove that:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \frac{a^3}{b^2+c^2}+\frac{b^3}{c^2+a^2}+\frac{c^3}{a^2+b^2} \ge \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}
        \]
    </p>
    <details>
        <summary>Hint 1</summary>
        <p>Rewrite each term so that Titu's Lemma can be applied:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \frac{a^3}{b^2+c^2}=\frac{a^4}{a(b^2+c^2)}
            \]
        </p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Hint 2</summary>
        <p>Find an upper bound for:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \Delta=a(b^2+c^2)+b(c^2+a^2)+c(a^2+b^2)
            \]
        </p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Solution</summary>
        <p>We first rewrite the expression and apply Titu's Lemma:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \begin{aligned}
                \frac{a^3}{b^2+c^2}+\frac{b^3}{c^2+a^2}+\frac{c^3}{a^2+b^2}
                &=\frac{a^4}{a(b^2+c^2)}+\frac{b^4}{b(c^2+a^2)}+\frac{c^4}{c(a^2+b^2)} \\
                &\overbrace{\ge}^{\text{Titu's}} \frac{(a^2+b^2+c^2)^2}{a(b^2+c^2)+b(c^2+a^2)+c(a^2+b^2)} \\
                &=\frac{1}{\Delta},
                \end{aligned}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>where:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \Delta=a(b^2+c^2)+b(c^2+a^2)+c(a^2+b^2).
            \]
        </p>
        <p>We can write \(\Delta\) as a scalar product and apply the Cauchy-Bunyakovsky-Schwarz inequality:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \begin{aligned}
                \Delta
                &=(ab)b+(ac)c+(bc)c+(ab)a+(ac)a+(bc)b, \\
                \Delta^2
                &\le \left[2(a^2b^2+a^2c^2+b^2c^2)\right]
                \left[2(a^2+b^2+c^2)\right] \\
                &=4(a^2b^2+a^2c^2+b^2c^2).
                \end{aligned}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Since \(a^2+b^2+c^2=1\), we also have:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                a^2b^2+a^2c^2+b^2c^2
                \le \frac{(a^2+b^2+c^2)^2}{3}=\frac{1}{3}.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Therefore \(\Delta^2\le\frac{4}{3}\), so \(\Delta\le\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\). Combining this estimate with Titu's Lemma gives:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \frac{a^3}{b^2+c^2}+\frac{b^3}{c^2+a^2}+\frac{c^3}{a^2+b^2}
                \ge \frac{1}{\Delta}
                \ge \frac{1}{2/\sqrt{3}}
                =\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Equality holds when \(a=b=c=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\).</p>
    </details>
