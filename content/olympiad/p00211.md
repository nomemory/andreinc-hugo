<div class="mp" id="p00211">
    <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00211/">Problem 00211</a></p>
    <p>Let \(a,b,c\) be positive real numbers such that \(ab+bc+ca=3\). Prove that:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            (a^2+2)(b^2+2)(c^2+2) \ge 27.
        \]
    </p>
    <details>
        <summary>Hint 1</summary>
        <p>Set \(s=a+b+c\) and \(p=abc\), then expand the product in terms of \(s\) and \(p\).</p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Hint 2</summary>
        <p>Use the condition and AM-GM to prove that \(s\ge3\) and \(p\le1\).</p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Solution</summary>
        <p>Let \(s=a+b+c\) and \(p=abc\). First, from the condition and the well-known inequality \(a^2+b^2+c^2\ge ab+bc+ca\), we obtain:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                s^2=a^2+b^2+c^2+2(ab+bc+ca)\ge3+6=9,
            \]
        </p>
        <p>so \(s\ge3\).</p>
        <p>Applying AM-GM to \(ab\), \(bc\), and \(ca\) gives:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                1=\frac{ab+bc+ca}{3}\ge\sqrt[3]{a^2b^2c^2}=p^{2/3},
            \]
        </p>
        <p>hence \(p\le1\).</p>
        <p>Now expand the expression:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \begin{aligned}
                (a^2+2)(b^2+2)(c^2+2)
                &=p^2+2(a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2)+4(a^2+b^2+c^2)+8.
                \end{aligned}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Using \(ab+bc+ca=3\), we have:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2
                =(ab+bc+ca)^2-2abc(a+b+c)=9-2ps
            \]
        </p>
        <p>and:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                a^2+b^2+c^2=s^2-2(ab+bc+ca)=s^2-6.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Therefore:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \begin{aligned}
                (a^2+2)(b^2+2)(c^2+2)
                &=p^2+2(9-2ps)+4(s^2-6)+8 \\
                &=(2s-p)^2+2.
                \end{aligned}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Finally, since \(s\ge3\) and \(p\le1\), we get \(2s-p\ge6-1=5\). Consequently:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                (a^2+2)(b^2+2)(c^2+2)=(2s-p)^2+2\ge25+2=27.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Equality holds when \(a=b=c=1\).</p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Source</summary>

    </details>
</div>
