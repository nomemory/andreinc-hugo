<div class="mp" id="p00213"> 
    <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00213/">Problem 00213</a></p>
    <p>Compute the sum:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            S = \frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \log_2 i} + \frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\log_3 i} + \cdots + \frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\log_n i}
        \]
    </p>
    <details>
    <summary>Solution</summary>
    <p>We use the identities:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \log_a x + \log_a y = \log_a(xy),
            \qquad
            \frac{1}{\log_a b} = \log_b a.
        \]
    </p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \begin{aligned}
                S &= \frac{1}{\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n}\log_2 i}
                   + \frac{1}{\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n}\log_3 i}
                   + \cdots
                   + \frac{1}{\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n}\log_n i} \\[1em]
                  &= \frac{1}{\log_2(n!)}
                   + \frac{1}{\log_3(n!)}
                   + \cdots
                   + \frac{1}{\log_n(n!)} \\[0.8em]
                  &= \log_{n!} 2 + \log_{n!} 3 + \cdots + \log_{n!} n
                   = \log_{n!}(n!) = 1.
            \end{aligned}
        \]
    </p>
    </details>
    <details>
    <summary>Source</summary>
    <p>Al. Leonte, C.Niculescu - Probleme de algebra si analiza Craiova 1981</p>
    </details>
