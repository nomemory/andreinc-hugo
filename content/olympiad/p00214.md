<div class="mp" id="p00214">
    <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00214/">Problem 00214</a></p>
    <p>Let \(x,y,z>0\), and let \(\log\) denote the base-10 logarithm. Prove that if:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            y = 10^{\frac{1}{1-\log x}}
            \qquad\text{and}\qquad
            z = 10^{\frac{1}{1-\log y}},
        \]
    </p>
    <p>then:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            x = 10^{\frac{1}{1-\log z}}.
        \]
    </p>
    <details>
        <summary>Solution</summary>
        <p>Taking logarithms in the first relation, we get:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \begin{aligned}
                    &\log y = \frac{1}{1-\log x} \\[0.5em]
                    &\Longleftrightarrow \log y\,(1-\log x) = 1 \\[0.5em]
                    &\Longleftrightarrow \log y-\log x\,\log y = 1 \\[0.5em]
                    &\Longleftrightarrow \log y-1 = \log x\,\log y \\[0.5em]
                    &\Longleftrightarrow \log x = \frac{\log y-1}{\log y}.
                \end{aligned}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Therefore:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                x = 10^{\frac{\log y-1}{\log y}}.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Similarly, the second relation gives:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \log y = \frac{\log z-1}{\log z}
                \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
                y = 10^{\frac{\log z-1}{\log z}}.
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Substituting into the expression for \(x\), we obtain:</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \begin{aligned}
                    x &= 10^{\frac{\frac{\log z-1}{\log z}-1}{\frac{\log z-1}{\log z}}} \\[1em]
                      &= 10^{-\frac{1}{\log z-1}}
                       = 10^{\frac{1}{1-\log z}}.
                \end{aligned}
            \]
        </p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Source</summary>
        <p>Al. Leonte, C.Niculescu - Probleme de algebra si analiza Craiova 1981</p>
    </details>
