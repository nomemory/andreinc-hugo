<div class="mp" id="p00216">
    <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00216/">Problem 00216</a></p>
    <p>Let \(a,b,c>0\) satisfy \(a^2+b^2=c^2\), with \(c+b\ne1\) and \(c-b\ne1\). Prove that:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            \log_{b+c}a + \log_{c-b}a
            = 2\log_{c+b}a\cdot\log_{c-b}a.
        \]
    </p>
    <details>
        <summary>Hint</summary>
        <p>\(a^2 = c^2 - b^2 = (c-b) \cdot (c+b)\)</p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Solution</summary>
        <p>If \(a=1\), both sides are \(0\). For \(a\ne1\):</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                a^2 = c^2-b^2 = (c-b)(c+b).
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Taking logarithms to base \(a\):</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \begin{aligned}
                    &2 = \log_a(c-b) + \log_a(c+b) \\[0.8em]
                    &\Longleftrightarrow
                    2 = \frac{1}{\log_{c-b}a} + \frac{1}{\log_{c+b}a} \\[1em]
                    &\Longleftrightarrow
                    \log_{c+b}a + \log_{c-b}a
                    = 2\log_{c+b}a\cdot\log_{c-b}a.
                \end{aligned}
            \]
        </p>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Source</summary>
        <p>Al. Leonte, C.Niculescu - Probleme de algebra si analiza Craiova 1981</p>
    </details>
</div>
