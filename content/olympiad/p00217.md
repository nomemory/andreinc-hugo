<div class="mp" id="p00217">
    <p><a class="mpl" href="/olympiad/p00217/">Problem 00217</a></p>
    <p>Let \(a,b>0\), with \(a\ne1\), \(b\ne1\), and \(ab\ne1\). Study the monotonicity of the function:</p>
    <p class="mpc">
        \[
            f(x) = \log_a x + \log_b x,
            \qquad x>0.
        \]
    </p>
    <p>Note: The problem can be solved without the use of derivatives.</p>
    <details>
        <summary>Solution</summary>
        <p>Changing the base to \(10\):</p>
        <p class="mpc">
            \[
                \begin{aligned}
                    f(x) &= \frac{\log x}{\log a} + \frac{\log x}{\log b} \\[0.8em]
                         &= \frac{\log a+\log b}{\log a\,\log b}\,\log x
                          = \frac{\log(ab)}{\log a\,\log b}\,\log x.
                \end{aligned}
            \]
        </p>
        <p>Since \(\log x\) is strictly increasing, we only need the sign of its coefficient:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>If \(a,b \gt 1\), then \(f\) is strictly increasing.</li>
            <li>If \(0 \lt a,b \lt 1\), then \(f\) is strictly decreasing.</li>
            <li>If one base is below \(1\) and the other above \(1\), the denominator is negative. Thus \(f\) is strictly increasing when \(ab<1\), and strictly decreasing when \(ab>1\).</li>
        </ul>
    </details>
    <details>
        <summary>Source</summary>
        <p>Al. Leonte, C.Niculescu - Probleme de algebra si analiza Craiova 1981</p>
    </details>
