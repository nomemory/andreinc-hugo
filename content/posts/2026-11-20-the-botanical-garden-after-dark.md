> The *original* pictures were taken with a OnePlus 15 phone, which is not exactly a camera flagship that behaves well in the dark. Because it was dark and the lights were soft.

There's something surreal about the Grădina Botanică in Bucharest after dark, especially when there's a [*Lumina Park* exhibition](https://lumina-park.ro/galerie/). I suspect the surrealism and the creepiness are involuntary in what should be a festival of light.
